The inbox for your AI coding agents

Your agents spend a good part of the day blocked, waiting on you. Mergeline is one inbox for Claude Code, Codex and Cursor that shows who is waiting and for how long, and puts the answer, the diff and the merge one click away.

Human wait time is the bottleneck

An agent that asks a question at 10:02 and gets an answer at 10:25 did nothing for 23 minutes. Mergeline puts every agent in one list, sorted by what it needs from you, and counts how long each one waited.

  1. Deploy

    Pick an agent and a task. Each agent works on a branch of its own.

  2. Get pinged

    Only when an agent needs an answer or has something to merge. Its question opens in plain words.

  3. Act

    Answer in one box, read the diff beside the list, Merge. No terminal juggling.

  4. Measure

    Every merge is a signed record: which agent and model, who reviewed it, how long it waited on you.

Why not the tools you already have?

They are good at running agents. Mergeline is about the person between them.

Answer from anywhere

The inbox works the same on a phone: the agent's question, one reply box, the diff and Merge. Its top line is the day's numbers: who is waiting on you now, the median wait, and what merged.

  • One command to start, signed in on your own computer, no settings to fill in.
  • No GitHub needed: review the local diff and merge on your machine, or open pull requests with checks when gh is there.
  • Runs on your laptop or your team's dev box. Nothing leaves it; anonymous usage numbers stay off unless you turn them on.
