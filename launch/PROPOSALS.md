# Proposals, October 2026

Every grant and hackathon Kipdeck should enter after Colosseum, with its form filled in field by field in the kit named in the last column. Written on 2026-10-14 from each program's own pages; the kits say what was read and what could not be opened (most forms sit behind a login, so their fields come from the published rules). Best bets first: cheapest to finish and most likely to pay, weighed against the deadline.

Kipdeck is built on agent-office (MIT, by webdevcody / AgentSystemLabs). Every kit credits upstream and keeps upstream's work and traction apart from ours.

One blocker sits on every row: written permission from Nortal for outside work, IP ownership of what is built and accepting prize or grant money. It is the first unticked box in each kit's eligibility checklist. Ask once, for all of them (task `nortal-permission` in [deadlines.json](deadlines.json)).
