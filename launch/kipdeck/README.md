## Monid

The brief quoted Monid (monid.ai) as having raised USD 7.7M. On 2026-10-07 its site described "Monid connects your agent to 1,700 tools & APIs": a marketplace where agents discover and pay for tools at runtime. The page says nothing about funding or investors, and no primary source for the figure was found. So the figure is dropped from the deck and the posts. Monid is also a different category (paying for tools, not people reviewing agents' work); if a deck needs a funded comparable, use one whose round is on its own blog or a filing, with the link.
