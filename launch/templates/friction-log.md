# Friction log

Amazon Build Ship Shape gives up to a 10 percent judging bonus for a friction log, and Nebius has a Most Valuable Feedback prize. Log as you go, not the night before: one row per thing that slowed you down, written the moment it happens.

Severity: 1 = cosmetic, 2 = cost time but had a workaround, 3 = blocked until fixed or worked around badly, 4 = could not finish the task.

Three to five sentences: the worst problems first, then what worked well. Paste into the product feedback field.
